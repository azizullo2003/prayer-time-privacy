Privacy Policy

Prayer Time Offline (Namoz Vaqti) · last updated 29 August 2026

The app has no account to sign into and no advertising. Prayer times are still worked out entirely on your phone. Since version 1.1 the app does send crash reports and a small amount of anonymous usage information — and never your location. This page says exactly what that means.

What changed in version 1.1

Versions 1.0 and earlier sent nothing at all. That had a cost we only discovered after release: two mistakes in the prayer-time calculation — one in Andijan, one in Riyadh — were found only because users noticed the app disagreed with the adhan and took the trouble to write in. Every other user affected by the same mistake simply had wrong times and no way for us to know.

Version 1.1 adds crash reporting and a small number of specific measurements so that the next such mistake is visible to us without anyone having to report it. We used the narrowest form of this we could build, and the rest of this page is the detail.

Your location never leaves the device

Prayer times and the qibla direction are geometry: both are calculated from your latitude and longitude and the position of the sun, entirely on your device. Your coordinates are stored only on the phone, in the app's own storage.

Your coordinates, your city and your country are never included in anything the app sends. This is enforced in the code, not just promised here: the functions that send information accept only fixed values — a calculation method, a school, yes or no — and have no way to carry a place name.

You can turn location off altogether and pick a city from a built-in list instead. The app then never asks the system for your position.

What the app sends

  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, we receive the technical details of the crash: where in the code it happened, the device model and the operating system version.
  • Performance data. How long the app takes to start and to draw its screens.
  • Which calculation convention is in use — the method and the school — and whether you changed it yourself rather than accepting the default. This is the measurement that matters: if many people in one region keep changing away from the default, the default for that region is wrong.
  • Taps on the calculation row on the main screen.

That is the complete list. There is no general activity logging: we do not receive which prayers you set alarms for, when you open the app, what you look at, or anything you type.

Identifiers

The app creates an anonymous sign-in with no name, email or password behind it. It exists for one reason: so that one phone crashing forty times can be told apart from forty phones crashing once. It cannot be traced back to you, and deleting the app ends it.

No advertising identifier is collected. The part of the analytics software that would read it is not included in the app at all, and there is no advertising, no ad network and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

Who processes it

Crash reports and the measurements above are processed by Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Anonymous Authentication) on our behalf, under Firebase's privacy terms. We operate no servers of our own. On Android the app now holds the INTERNET permission, which it did not before, solely for this.

City names

To show a city name rather than a pair of numbers, the app asks the operating system to turn coordinates into a place name — Apple's MapKit geocoding on iOS, the system geocoder on Android. That request is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Searching for a city by name in the place picker uses the same service. The timetable itself never needs it.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alarms and reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Nothing is pushed from a server, and no notification token is created or shared.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It asks for no personal information from anyone, including children, and the diagnostic data described above is not linked to any identity.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Any change that affects what data leaves your device will also be described in the app's release notes — as this one is.
