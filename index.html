Privacy Policy

Prayer Time Offline (Namoz Vaqti) · last updated 13 August 2026

This app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What the app does with your location

Prayer times and the qibla direction are geometry: both are calculated from your latitude and longitude and the position of the sun. That calculation happens entirely on your device. Your coordinates are stored only on the phone, in the app's own storage, so the timetable is correct the next time you open it.

You can turn location off altogether and pick a city from a built-in list instead. The app then never asks the system for your position.

The one thing that leaves the device

To show a city name rather than a pair of numbers, the app asks the operating system to turn coordinates into a place name. On iOS this uses Apple's MapKit geocoding service; on Android it uses the system geocoder. That request is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, and it is the only feature in the app that uses the network.

  • It is used for the city label only. The timetable itself never needs it.
  • Searching for a city by name in the place picker uses the same service.
  • Nothing is sent to us, and we operate no servers. On Android the app does not even hold the INTERNET permission.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alarms and reminders are scheduled locally by your device. Nothing is pushed from a server, and no notification token is created or shared.

Data we collect

None. We receive no data about you at all — not your location, not your settings, not usage statistics, not crash reports. Your settings stay on your device and in your own iCloud or Google device backup, which we cannot read.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Any change that affects what data leaves your device will also be described in the app's release notes.
